Global spectators connect a websocket to /global and receive the full snapshot as JSON after every event. Unlike the players' frames this is the SPECTATOR projection: every room, every purse and every shop's books at once, because the replay is where the audience gets to see the whole town.

Shape: {"type":"state","game":"cogmud","world":{"rooms":[{"id","name","desc","x","y","dark","exits":[int]}],"items":[{"id","name","plural","value"}],"npcs":[{"id","name","room","trade":[int]}]},"seats":[{"seat","name","room","coin","items":[6],"carried","questPoints","delivered":[2],"quests":[{"item","count","delivered"}],"retainerOf","retainerTurns","robberies","robbed","score","pending","scripted","sentence","say","notes"} x6],"rooms":[{"id","items":[6],"cogs":[seat],"log":[str]} x9],"npcs":[{"id","room","coin","stock":[6],"ask":[6],"bid":[6]} x5],"offers":[{"kind":"trade|hire","from","to","item","qty","coin","postedTurn"}],"chronicle":[{"seat","sentence","intent","reason","salience"}],"town":{"coinInPlay","delivered","robberies","trades"},"turn","turns","turnsPlayed","phase":"turn|done","gameDone","reason","policyNames":[str],"events":[...],"started","done","connected":[bool]}.

The events array is append-only and is the whole replay language - the viewer re-derives every frame from it plus the seed:
  {"kind":"start"} - everything else is re-derived from the seed and the constant world.
  {"kind":"turn","turn":int,"rooms":[9 x [6 ground counts]],"npcs":[5 x {stock,coin}],"cogs":[6 x {room,prevRoom,coin,items,delivered,retainerOf,retainerTurns,robberies,robbed}]} - the world at the turn's open, and for the trailing one at the episode's end. These are DERIVED: a replay recomputes them from the seed plus the act events and rejects a recording that disagrees.
  {"kind":"act","turn","seat","order" (initiative 0..5),"intent" ("move|take|drop|buy|sell|give|trade|accept|hire|rob|say|quest|wait|none"),"room","toRoom","item","qty","npc","other","coin","reason","salience" (0..100),"sentence" (verbatim, <= 240 runes),"say" (<= 160 runes),"text" (the seat's notes),"scripted"} - one per seat per turn, appended IN RESOLUTION ORDER, so the log order is the class-then-initiative order the rules fix.
  {"kind":"end","turn":turns played,"text":"complete|deadline"}.

The complete outcome vocabulary on an act event: ok, waited, unparsed, no_verb, no_target, ambiguous_target, no_such_exit, no_such_item, not_carrying, carry_limit, no_npc_here, not_wanted, out_of_stock, cannot_afford, npc_broke, no_matching_commission, no_such_cog, not_in_room, self_target, no_such_offer, offer_expired, bound_by_contract, robbery_failed, nothing_to_take, thievery_forbidden, rejected.

Salience drives the highlight reel, which is chosen by event salience rather than tick order: robbery succeeded 100, a commission completed by this delivery 90, robbery attempted and failed 80, hire accepted 70, trade offer accepted 65, commission partly delivered 60, a buy or sell moving >= 20 coin 45, a trade or hire offer posted 40, a gift between cogs 35, a buy or sell moving < 20 coin 25, a spoken line 20 (30 when it runs past 40 characters), asking a keeper about a commission 15, a take or drop 15, a move 10, any no-op 5.

Seats play under anonymous cog aliases; the payload carries "policyNames" alongside, spectator-side only, and the viewer swaps the real names in wherever a name is RENDERED - including inside the chronicle's verbatim sentences - while the recorded bytes keep the alias. The browser page at /client/global renders the parchment map live; /client/replay plays a recorded episode; and the STATIC replay viewer bundle renders replays hosted by the platform at index.html?replay=<url>, re-deriving every frame in the browser from the recorded events with the same Nim sim the server played the episode with.
