cogmud.player.v1 - JSON text frames over the websocket named by COWORLD_PLAYER_WS_URL (already carrying ?slot=N&token=T). A Cogmud policy is a PROMPT: the player container's only job is to deliver it; the game server makes every decision by sending that prompt plus the seat's whole observation to Claude once a turn, as one parallel batch of six.

game->player frames:
  {"type":"welcome","protocol":"cogmud.player.v1","slot":N,"name":"Gizmo","room":"The Chapel","turns":14} on connect.
  {"type":"state",...} after every event, REDACTED to the seat's own view because information in this town is strictly local. Shape: {"type":"state","slot":N,"name":str,"coin":int,"items":[{"item":str,"count":int,"value":int}],"carried":int,"carryLimit":8,"quests":[{"item":str,"count":int,"delivered":int,"points":int,"outstanding":int,"settledBy":"Guildmaster Vell","at":"The Guildhall"}],"room":{"id":int,"name":str,"desc":str,"dark":bool,"exits":[{"id":int,"name":str}],"ground":[{"item":str,"count":int}],"cogs":[alias],"npc":{"name":str,"coin":int,"goods":[{"item":str,"stock":int,"ask":int,"bid":int}]}},"heard":[str],"offers":[{"kind":"trade|hire","from":alias,"item":str,"qty":int,"coin":int}],"standing":{"hiredTo":str,"hiredToTurns":int,"retainers":[{"name":str,"turns":int}]},"map":[{"name":str,"exits":[str],"npc":str}],"lastSentence":str,"lastOutcome":str,"notes":str,"turn":int,"turns":int,"turnsPlayed":int,"started":bool,"done":bool,"reason":str}. NO other seat's coin, pack, commissions, notes or score; nothing at all happening in another room; no shopkeeper's stock or prices but the one in the room. The map is public knowledge - every room's name, exits and keeper - because it is in any MUD. Decisions are server-side, so the redaction loses nothing.
  {"type":"final","done":true,"slot":N,"scores":[6],"names":[6 aliases],"wealth":[6],"questPoints":[6],"turns":int,"reason":"complete|deadline"} at episode end, after which the player should exit.

player->game frames:
  {"type":"prompt","prompt":str,"scripted":str} - the prompt (max 4000 runes, cut on a rune boundary) IS the policy, sent right after connecting and again after welcome (a race guard). "scripted" selects a built-in baseline instead: "factor" (or "1"/"true"/"yes") plays the competent quest-and-trade agent, "magpie" (or "thief") the thief-peddler, "" means LLM-driven. The reference player reads PLAYER_PROMPT and PLAYER_SCRIPTED from its environment.

The reply the server asks Claude for, every turn, is one JSON object: {"action":"one sentence of plain English","say":"one line spoken aloud, or \"\"","notes":"..."}. "action" is REQUIRED and capped at 240 runes; "say" is optional, capped at 160 runes, and forced to "" when the speech flag is off; "notes" is optional and capped at 600 runes, private to the seat and fed back verbatim every turn. Unknown keys are ignored. A reply that is not a JSON object, or whose "action" is missing or empty, is INVALID: it is retried once in a smaller batch carrying a hint, and then falls back to the scripted baseline. A sentence that IS well-formed JSON but whose prose the grammar cannot read is NOT invalid - it resolves as a legal no-op with a recorded reason that the seat is told, verbatim, in its next observation. That is how a policy learns the grammar, and it is why no action menu is needed.

A policy is just a prompt: field one by reusing the published cogmud-player runnable with PLAYER_PROMPT set to your strategy.
